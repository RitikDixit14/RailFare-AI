the dynamic pricing problem

Most of rail pricing is guesswork.

Official Indian Railways Flexi-fare circulars mandate a 10% price jump for every 10% block of seats sold, capped at 1.50x base fare on premium Rajdhani and Shatabdi fleets. Yet passengers book blindly without seeing where a train sits in its surge cycle.

RailFare AI extracts live berth quotas, computes exact occupancy velocity, and runs Random Forest regression to project fares up to 7 days ahead.
